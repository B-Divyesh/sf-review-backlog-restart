Privacy field note · August 27, 2026

Your study history stays yours.

Review Backlog Restart is built to work without an account, a server upload, or surveillance.

What is stored

When you import a file, its card fields, your recovery settings, selected route, and check-ins are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB on this device. Processing happens locally in your browser.

What leaves your device

Nothing from your imported deck or plan is transmitted by this app. There are no analytics, advertising trackers, external fonts, third-party scripts, or cookies. The hosting provider may keep ordinary security logs such as IP address and request time.

Your controls

You can export your plan as JSON or CSV at any time. “Delete local data” removes the current plan from this browser. Clearing browser storage also removes it.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
