Privacy for your local plan data

This planner works without an account or a deck upload.

What this browser stores

When you import a file, card fields, settings, the selected plan, and daily records are stored in this browser’s IndexedDB. The sample demo uses a separate browser database.

What leaves this device

This app does not send imported cards or plan data to an application server. It has no analytics, advertising trackers, external fonts, third-party scripts, or cookies. The host may keep ordinary security logs such as IP address and request time.

Your controls

Export a plan as JSON or CSV at any time. Start over removes the real plan from this browser. Leaving the demo removes its separate sample plan.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
