Terms field note · August 27, 2026

A plan, not a promise.

This tool provides transparent workload estimates to help you decide how to return to an overdue deck.

Use of the tool

Review Backlog Restart is free and provided as-is. You may use its output for personal study planning. The app does not modify or sync your Anki collection.

Estimates

Risk scores and schedules are planning estimates based only on the columns you import. They are not FSRS predictions, memory measurements, or retention guarantees. Your actual review time and recall will vary.

Your files

You remain responsible for your source deck, backups, and any action you take in Anki. Inspect exported action lists before applying tags or changing your study workflow.

Availability

The app may change or stop being offered. Export your plan if you need a durable copy.
