Terms for using this planner

This free tool gives workload estimates for returning to an overdue review deck.

Use of the tool

You may use the exported action list for personal study planning. The app does not modify or sync an Anki collection.

Estimates

Risk scores and schedules use only the columns you import. They are not FSRS predictions, memory measurements, or retention guarantees. Review time and recall vary.

Your files

You remain responsible for your source deck, backups, and changes you make in Anki. Inspect an exported action list before applying tags or changing study steps.

Availability

The app may change or stop being offered. Export JSON if you need a durable copy.
